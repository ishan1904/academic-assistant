Welcome to Your AI-Powered Academic Assistant!

This platform simplifies studying, teaching, and professional tasks with the power of artificial intelligence (AI). Whether a student, educator, or professional, various documents—such as syllabi, chapters, resumes, or presentation slides—can be analyzed to generate useful insights and tailored outputs. Eliminate manual effort as AI streamlines the process, making tasks more efficient and effective.

What You Can Do:

  • Syllabus Enhancement: Get AI-powered recommendations to improve syllabus structure, clarity, and effectiveness. Insights help align the syllabus with student needs, industry trends, and academic best practices, ensuring a well-organized and engaging course outline.
  • Chapter Insights: Extract key concepts and generate practice questions from a chapter (PDF or PPT). This helps in breaking down complex topics for better understanding, making it a valuable tool for both studying and teaching.
  • Recommendation Letter Assistance: Generate a professional recommendation letter based on a resume (.pdf or .docx). This helps in crafting a strong, well-structured letter, making it ideal for job applications.
  • Exam Questions Generation: Generate unique exam questions from PowerPoint slides (.pptx). This helps in creating effective assessments, making it a useful tool for both teachers and students.

How to Get Started: Pick an option below, upload your file, choose an AI model (OpenAI or Claude), and click a button to see the magic happen.
