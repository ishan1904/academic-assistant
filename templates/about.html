About AI Academic Assistant

AI-powered tools for educators and students

🎯

Our mission

We built AI Academic Assistant to reduce the time educators spend on repetitive tasks — grading, question generation, exam preparation — so they can focus on what matters: teaching.

For students, our tools support active learning by extracting key concepts, generating practice questions, and building research briefs from course materials.

🛠️

How it works

The Platform uses a multi-provider AI architecture. Grading is powered by Groq's LLaMA 3.1 (fast and cost-efficient), with fallback to OpenAI and Anthropic Claude. OCR for scanned documents uses Google Gemini. All results are cached so repeated requests are instant.

Built with Python (Flask), PostgreSQL, and SQLAlchemy — deployed on Railway.

🔒

Privacy and safety

We don't sell your data. Uploaded files are deleted after processing. Grading results are cached using a cryptographic hash of the content — not tied to your identity.

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Sessions use HttpOnly, SameSite cookies. Read our full Privacy Policy.

⚠️

AI limitations

AI-generated content — grading feedback, questions, research briefs — should always be reviewed by a qualified human. Language models can make mistakes, hallucinate references, or misinterpret context.

This platform is a productivity tool, not a replacement for professional academic judgment.

What you can do

Grading Auto-grade student submissions against an answer key. Supports MCQ, short-answer, and long-answer with configurable strictness.
Questions Generate MCQ, True/False, and open-ended questions from uploaded PDFs or slides.
Concepts Extract core concepts and definitions from chapter documents for quick revision.
Exam papers Generate multiple unique exam papers from lecture slides with configurable question counts.
Research Produce structured research briefs with executive summary, key findings, data, and sources.
Letters Generate professional recommendation letters from uploaded resumes.
Citations Format citations in APA, MLA, Chicago, IEEE, and Harvard styles.
Syllabus Analyse course syllabi and receive structured improvement suggestions.
