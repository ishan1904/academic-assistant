Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2025

1. Overview

AI Academic Assistant ("the Platform", "we", "us") is an educational tool that uses artificial intelligence to support students and educators. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data. We take privacy seriously — particularly because educational records are protected under FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act) and, where applicable, GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation).

2. What data we collect

Account data

When you sign up, we store your username, email address, and a bcrypt-hashed password. We never store your password in plain text.

Uploaded documents

Files you upload (PDFs, PPTX, DOCX, TXT) are processed to extract text for AI analysis. Uploaded files are stored temporarily during processing and are deleted automatically after your request completes. Extracted text may be cached (see Section 3) to reduce repeated AI costs.

AI-generated content

Prompts sent to third-party AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, Google Gemini) include extracted text from your uploads. These providers have their own privacy policies which govern how they handle data in transit. We do not sell your data to these providers for training purposes.

Usage data

We may log request metadata (timestamps, feature used, file size) for debugging and reliability purposes. These logs do not contain document content.

3. Caching and data retention

To reduce costs and improve speed, AI grading results and generated content are cached in our database using a cryptographic hash of the input content. This means:

  • The same document processed twice will return the cached result.
  • Cached results do not contain your personal identity — only a hash of the content.
  • Cache entries expire after 30 days and are then cleared automatically.

If you need cached results removed sooner, contact us at the address in Section 8.

4. Third-party AI providers

Depending on the feature used, your document content may be sent to one or more of these providers:

  • OpenAI — gpt-4o-mini (question generation, syllabus analysis, exam papers)
  • Anthropic (Claude) — available as an alternative grading engine
  • Groq (LLaMA 3.1) — primary grading engine (fast, low-cost)
  • Google Gemini — OCR fallback for scanned PDFs

Each provider's data handling is governed by their respective privacy policies. We recommend reviewing these if you handle sensitive student records.

5. FERPA considerations

If you are an educator using this platform to grade student submissions, please be aware:

  • Student answer papers may constitute educational records under FERPA.
  • You should obtain appropriate institutional consent before uploading identifiable student work.
  • We recommend anonymising student submissions (remove names, student IDs) before uploading.
  • Graded JSON output files are available for download and are your responsibility to manage.

6. Cookies and sessions

We use a single session cookie to maintain your login state. This cookie is:

  • HttpOnly — not accessible to JavaScript
  • SameSite=Lax — protects against cross-site request forgery
  • Set to expire after 1 hour of inactivity
  • Marked Secure in production (HTTPS only)

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Correction — update your username or email via account settings
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object — opt out of any processing beyond essential service delivery
